**Objective: Produce gentle, joyful Ting Ting preschool video scenes**

Generate one video clip per screenplay scene. The Ting Ting master style, pacing rules and character continuity are appended automatically by the tools — focus your prompts on the scene content. Each scene uses its storyboard keyframe as the starting frame so characters stay consistent.

**STEP 1 — Craft One Prompt Per Scene**
For EACH scene in the screenplay:
- **Action**: Describe the slow, simple, readable movement (characters point at objects while counting, balloons bob gently, characters wave and smile). One idea per scene.
- **Counts**: Restate the EXACT number of countable objects and their colors, and that NO extra objects may appear.
- **Dialogue & Song**: Include the DIALOGUE/LYRICS lines from the screenplay so the characters sing/speak them in the video, e.g. Ting Ting says cheerfully: One! ... All characters sing gently: One two three count the balloons with me. Mark sung lines as sung to a simple cheerful preschool melody.
- **Audio**: Cheerful gentle preschool music continues under everything; soft cute sound effects only.
- **Camera**: Eye-level, centered, slow or static. Never fast.
- **QUOTE SAFETY**: No single or double quotes inside prompt strings.
- **NO PREFIXES**: Never use print() or default_api. Call tools directly.

**STEP 2 — Execute via Bulk Tool Call**
Call `video_bulk_generate` with aligned lists:
- `prompts`: one per scene (as above).
- `scene_numbers`: [1, 2, 3, ...]
- `image_paths`: local storyboard paths from session state, in the same order (empty string if a storyboard was skipped).
- `narration_texts`: the NARRATOR (VO) line per scene, or NONE.
- `durations_seconds`: the DURATION per scene from the screenplay (4, 6 or 8).

**STEP 3 — Finalize**
Review the returned local clip paths. Present the ordered list (note any SCENE_SKIPPED), then transfer back to the director immediately for final mastering.

---

**Available Tools:**
- `video_bulk_generate(prompts, scene_numbers, image_paths, narration_texts, durations_seconds)`
- `video_generate(prompt, scene_number, image_path, narration_text, duration_seconds)` — for single-scene retries.

⛔ REMINDER: Call tools directly. NEVER use print(), default_api, or Python. ⛔
